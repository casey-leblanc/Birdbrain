# What changed since Birdbrain 1.1.2

This is the experimental 2.0, on the `birdbrain-experimental` branch. To try it, see [TESTING.md](TESTING.md).

## No AI, and nothing to set up

- There is no AI in Birdbrain any more: no Jev, no key, no account. Plain rules decide what each thing is.
- If the rules get one wrong, say what it is once (press E on the card) and it sticks, for that card and the others like it.
- A lab called "Oxidase Test" is no longer listed as an exam, and "Exam 2 Review" is not the exam.
- First run takes under a minute: LSU is already chosen, so there is nothing to type before signing in.
- Birdbrain can start with your computer, so reminders arrive without opening it.

## The list is the app

- The header and its summaries are gone. The window is your list: now, this week, later.
- One small dot in the corner says what Birdbrain is doing; one menu holds everything else.
- Each day says its load beside its name ("3 due · 1 exam"), and a heavy day ahead is called out once, early.
- When something goes wrong (offline, a site needs signing in to, a page that changed), one line says what and what to do.

## Adding your own things

- Quick write: press Space, type one line ("dentist tue 3pm #appointment"), press Enter. Under five seconds, no mouse.
- On Windows, Ctrl+Alt+Space opens quick write from anywhere, even with the window closed.
- Labels for things that aren't classes: Life, Extracurricular, Appointment, Work, and your own.
- Repeating items: "gym every mon wed 6pm". Tick one off and the next appears.
- Click a label or class tag to show only those.

## Moving things around

- Drag a card to reorder its day, to plan it for an earlier day, or onto Completed or Archived.
- A card from Moodle can't be planned later than it is due; your own cards can go anywhere.
- The same with the keyboard: M picks a card up, arrows move it, Enter drops it.
- Snooze a card until tomorrow, next week or a day you pick. It comes back by itself, never after its deadline.
- Every change has Undo for six seconds, and Ctrl+Z.

## Finding and doing

- Ctrl+K (or /) opens one line that searches your cards and runs commands: scan, settings, night, new label.
- Every action has a key; press ? to see them all.

## Reminders

- Notifications on your computer, on from the start: a morning list, "Due tonight" in the evening, one before an exam or quiz.
- Each is one short line, never a pile of them. On Windows, clicking one opens that card.
- Optional, under Settings › Advanced: the same lines on your phone through a Telegram bot of your own, with a Done button.

## Look and feel

- Cards lift, glide and spring into place; on Glass, a ripple of light answers a tick or a drop.
- Motion has three levels: Full, Subtle, Off.
- A text size setting. Text is checked to be readable in every theme, day and night.
- Settings is in plain order, with everything most students never need folded away under Advanced.

## Looking after itself

- Birdbrain updates itself: Settings › Updates, one button. The version you had is kept in case.
- Report a problem makes a zip with your name, emails, course titles and addresses taken out, and shows you all of it first. Nothing is uploaded.
- Backup: export your own items and labels to a file, and import it on another computer.

## Mac

- Birdbrain now runs on a Mac (Apple silicon and Intel): its own window, a menu bar icon, the standard menu, Cmd for Ctrl.
- Not on the Mac yet: the shortcut that works from anywhere, and opening a card by clicking its notification.

## Left out on purpose

- Google Calendar sync.
- Anything that needs a paid account or a key.
